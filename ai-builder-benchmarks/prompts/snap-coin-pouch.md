# Snap Coin Pouch

Create a simple snap coin pouch template with a body, fold behavior, stitch lines, and snap placement.

Requirements:

- AI Builder JSON only.
- Units in millimeters.
- Include at least one closed pattern piece for the pouch body.
- Use a fold line for the flap/body fold, with reasonable leather thickness and radius.
- Include seam allowance for the body.
- Include stitch paths along the side seams.
- Add two snap hardware markers that align as a closing pair.
- Use practical dimensions for a small pouch, roughly 150-170 mm wide and 120-150 mm total unfolded height.
- Use stable snake_case IDs and mark-line text labels.
