# Card Sleeve

Create a compact vertical card sleeve pattern for hand stitching.

Requirements:

- AI Builder JSON only.
- Units in millimeters.
- Two pattern pieces: a back panel and a slightly lower front pocket panel.
- Use rectangular cut boundaries so the compiled piece boundaries are closed.
- Include seam allowances on both pieces.
- Include stitch paths along the left, right, and bottom edges.
- Include seam connections between matching back/front left, right, and bottom edges.
- Use a 4 mm stitch pitch, slanted/diamond stitch holes, and stable snake_case IDs.
- Keep the pattern practical for credit cards: around 72 mm wide, 105 mm tall for the back, and 72 mm wide, 78 mm tall for the front.
- Add small text labels as mark lines for piece names.
